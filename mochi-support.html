🍡 Mochi Merge Bakery: Support

Questions about the bakery? Email ramosenterprisesinc@gmail.com with "Mochi Merge" in the subject, plus your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Baking tips

How does merging work?

Drag one item onto a matching item to merge them into the next item in its chain. Fill customer orders to earn coins and rebuild Harbor Street.

My board is full. What can I do?

Drag an item below the board to stash it in the Pantry. Tap it in the Pantry to bring it back. The Pantry starts with 4 slots and grows as you level up.

When do new chains unlock?

The Tea Bush unlocks at level 4 and the Cocoa Tree at level 7.

What is the Recipe Book?

It records every item you have made. Claim the coins for new entries, and finish a page for a bonus.

How do I turn sounds off?

Tap the mute button in the game.

Where is my bakery saved?

Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts, and we cannot restore a bakery. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does the game have purchases or ads?

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases and no ads.
