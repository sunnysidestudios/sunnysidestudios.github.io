🍡 Mochi Merge Bakery: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone game Mochi Merge Bakery, made by Sunny Side Studios ("we"). Mochi Merge Bakery collects no data.

What the game stores

Your board, Pantry, Recipe Book, coins, level and sound setting are saved in a file on your iPhone. That file never leaves your device and we never receive it.

No accounts, no internet

The game has no sign-in. It does not need an internet connection to play and does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

No ads, analytics or tracking

No advertising, no third-party analytics, and no advertising identifier. The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

Purchases

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases. If we add any later, Apple will process them and we will update this page first.

Children

The game is made for all ages. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
