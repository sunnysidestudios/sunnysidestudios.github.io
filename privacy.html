Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy covers games published by Sunny Side Studios ("we"), including Sunny Side Sudoku and Critter Rescue Sanctuary, and this website. We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not run ads or third-party tracking.

Information on your device

Game progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them.

Optional sign-in and iCloud (Sunny Side Sudoku)

Signing in with Apple or Google is optional. If you sign in, your progress is backed up to your own iCloud account so it can be restored on your devices. We do not operate servers that store this data. You can delete it any time in Settings › Account › Delete Account & Sign-in Data.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Share codes (Critter Rescue Sanctuary)

Share codes contain only game data (like your ranger name and sanctuary layout) and are shared only if you copy and send them yourself. Please don't put personal information in your ranger name.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game which items you own.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Our games contain no advertising, no third-party analytics and do not use the advertising identifier. This website uses no cookies or trackers.

Children

Our games are designed for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
