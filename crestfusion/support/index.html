Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

In the game, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. The starter pack comes back, and any Monthly Pass or 7-day pack time that is still active is rebuilt from your App Store purchase dates. Gems are consumable, so gems you already spent cannot be restored.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, with automatic backup copies. If iCloud is on for your Apple ID, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud storage so it can move to a new device. We never see or store your save.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, or use Hero > Save & Help > Export save code and paste the code into Import save code on the new device. If two saves differ, the game asks which one to keep.

My progress looks wrong after an update or crash.

The game keeps the newest intact copy and two backups, and it tells you if it restored a backup. If something still looks wrong, email us before you reinstall.

Do I need the internet?

No. The game works fully offline. Purchases and iCloud sync need a connection.

What do purchases include?

Every offer shows exactly what you get and the price before you buy. There are no random rewards for money. The Monthly Pass and the 7-day pack do not renew automatically; they simply end after 30 or 7 days.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Reminders are off unless you turn them on in Hero > Save & Help. There is at most one a day. You can also turn them off in iOS Settings > Notifications.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove the save on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. We hold no account or personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete that email.
