Crest Fusion Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Applies to the iPhone app Crest Fusion: Idle RPG by Sunny Side Studios (MOA RAMOS INC).

Summary: we do not collect your data

Crest Fusion does not collect, store, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. This matches the app's App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

  • Game save: stored on your iPhone, with backup copies.
  • iCloud copy: if iCloud is on, a copy of your save and your pass expiry dates is stored in your own iCloud key-value storage. It belongs to your Apple ID; we cannot access it.
  • Play statistics such as days played and records are counted on your device only and never sent anywhere.
  • Reminders (optional, off by default) are scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks Apple-signed purchase records on your device to deliver and restore items.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply, keep it no longer than 12 months, and delete it sooner on request.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no personal data from the app, so there is nothing stored with us to retain or delete. To delete game data: delete the app (removes the device save) and remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. To delete support emails, write to ramosenterprisesinc@gmail.com.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no chat and no way to contact other players.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change applies.
