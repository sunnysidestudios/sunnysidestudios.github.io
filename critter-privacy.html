🦦 Critter Rescue Sanctuary: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone game Critter Rescue Sanctuary and its widget, made by Sunny Side Studios ("we"). We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

On your device

Your rescued critters, sanctuary layout, ranger name and settings are stored on your iPhone. The home-screen widget reads the same on-device data. None of it is sent to us.

Ranger name and share codes

Your ranger name stays on your device unless you put it in a share code. Share codes hold only game data and go only where you send them yourself. Please do not put personal information in your ranger name.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

No sign-in, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no advertising identifier.

Purchases

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases. If we add any later, Apple will process them, we will never see your payment details, and we will update this page first.

Children

The game is made for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
