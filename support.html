Support

Need help with one of our games? Email ramosenterprisesinc@gmail.com. Please tell us which game, your device model, your iOS version and the app version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-game Shop and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the link: Sunny Side Sudoku or Critter Rescue Sanctuary.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. In Sunny Side Sudoku you can optionally sign in to back up progress to your own iCloud. Deleting the app removes local progress.

How do I delete my sign-in data (Sunny Side Sudoku)?

Settings › Account › Delete Account & Sign-in Data.

How do I turn off music or sounds?

Use the game's Settings screen (or the speaker button in Sunny Side Sudoku).
