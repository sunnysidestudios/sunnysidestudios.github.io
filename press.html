Mochi Merge Bakery

Short blurb

Mochi Merge Bakery is a cozy, free merge puzzle for iPhone from Sunny Side Studios. Merge flour, berries, milk, matcha and cocoa into 40 treats, fill chibi neighbors' orders, and rebuild Grandma's seaside bakery on Harbor Street. No ads, no purchases, no account, fully offline.

Long description

Mochi has inherited her grandma's little bakery, and it needs a lot of love. Tap the Flour Sack, Berry Bush and Milk Cow to bake ingredients, drag matching treats together to merge them, and serve customers for coins that rebuild Harbor Street one fix at a time. Five treat families and 40 treats, a Recipe Book with rewards, a Pantry for busy boards, a daily gift that never punishes a missed day, and generous stamina. Every piece of art is drawn in code — no AI art. Version 1.0 has no ads, no in-app purchases and collects no data.

Critter Rescue Sanctuary

Short blurb

Critter Rescue Sanctuary is a cozy, free walk-around rescue game for iPhone from Sunny Side Studios. Rescue stuck Florida critters, nurse them in rehab pens, ride out heat waves and storms, and release them home. Every release sends a postcard and opens a new trail. Offline, no ads, no account.

Long description

Start in the Mangrove with a crate, boots and a lot of heart. Spot a critter in trouble, tiptoe up so you don't spook it, and carry it to the pens. Build shade before the heat wave and drainage before the storm, plant natives to draw new visitors, then walk your healed patient to the release dock. Six habitats open one by one — Mangrove, Springs Run, Open Prairie, Backyard, Wetland and Barrier Island — with 16 original critters. Gentle threats, nothing lost for good. Fair by design: no loot boxes, gems or timers to skip; optional purchases are cosmetic, shown in real money, behind a parental gate, and every legendary can be earned free. The optional in-game radio plays AI-assisted Sunny Side Hustle songs.
