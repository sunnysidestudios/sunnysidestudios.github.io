🦦 Critter Rescue Sanctuary: Support

Need help with your sanctuary? Email ramosenterprisesinc@gmail.com with "Critter Rescue" in the subject, plus your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Ranger questions

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the Critter Rescue Sanctuary beta link.

Where is my sanctuary saved?

On your iPhone. There are no accounts. Deleting the app deletes your sanctuary.

What are share codes?

A share code is a short text that holds your ranger name and sanctuary layout. Copy it to send to a friend yourself. We never see it.

How do I turn off music or sounds?

Open the game's Settings screen.

Does the game have purchases or ads?

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases and no ads.
